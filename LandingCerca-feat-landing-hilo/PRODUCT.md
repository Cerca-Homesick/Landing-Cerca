# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

The landing page is the web surface for an iPhone app in development.

## Users

Anyone missing one specific person who lives far away: family, partners or friends, in any two cities. The page speaks to one sender and one recipient without naming the relationship. Visitors arrive curious and decide whether to join the waitlist.

## Product Purpose

CERCA lets someone share the small things of their day with one person far away. The landing page exists to make that idea felt and to collect waitlist signups. Success is a completed signup.

## Positioning

Sharing is told as old-fashioned post between two people: each small thing is a stamp, stamps are pasted on a letter the two people share, each send goes in an envelope, and a messenger pigeon carries it along a red route from one city to the other. Everything kept fills an album.

## Operating Context

Visitors read in Spanish (primary) or English, mostly expected on phones because the product is an iPhone app. This is an assumption, not analytics.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The app is in development and iPhone-only; it is not available yet.
- Every string ships in Spanish and English.
- Waitlist signup is the Jotform form `https://app.jotform.com/263007192678866?appEmbedded=1`, opened in a modal. Confirmation relies on the Jotform thank-you redirect to `?registro=confirmado`.
- The page must still show the envelope, pigeon route, stamps, the two-page letter and the album.
- Fonts stay self-hosted.

## Brand Commitments

- `CERCA-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` stays binding for palette, fonts, pigeons, envelope, stamps, postmark and wordmark, including its object names and voice. Its landing band order is not binding for this redesign.
- Name is always `CERCA`, in capitals. In Spanish copy use estampilla, carta, sobre, paloma, ruta, cuaderno, álbum, matasellos; never "momento", "timeline", "colección" or "hilo".
- Warm, handmade, a little playful, never techy. No emoji; the pigeon carries the emotion. Each pigeon pose has one meaning and is never decoration.
- Little text: about one sentence per scene, without section titles or numbered steps.

## Evidence on Hand

Sample illustrations in `src/assets/illustrations/` and pigeon poses in `src/assets/paloma/`. Monterrey–Naples, Valeria and Mamá are sample data and must be labeled as such. There are no testimonials, customers, usage numbers, pricing or launch dates, and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Two people first: the connection between them is the subject, not the feature list.
- Show the object doing its job instead of explaining it.
- Sample content is labeled honestly.
- Signup stays one tap away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor reduced motion as the design system specifies, keep full keyboard operation, and keep all content readable without animation.
